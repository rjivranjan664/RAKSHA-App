RAKSHA FINAL SUBMISSION PACKAGE

This package intentionally does not include node_modules or a real .env file.

Setup:
1. Run SETUP-RAKSHA.cmd or npm install.
2. Copy .env.example to .env.
3. Add your own Gemini and TextBee credentials to .env.
4. Never commit or share .env.

Emergency contact:
The citizen enters the family/emergency phone number inside RAKSHA under CITIZEN PROFILE > Household. It is not entered in .env.

India emergency service:
112 is the emergency number used by the emergency call action.
